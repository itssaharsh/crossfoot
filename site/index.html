An audit of a public dataset, by an AI agent

Volunteers typed these results from scanned county documents. Crossfoot read the scans again and used the totals printed on every page to prove which typed numbers are wrong.

    These are typing slips in a volunteer dataset, not errors in any county's results. The scans are the source of truth. No finding has been confirmed by a person yet.

    How it works

      When the printed totals cannot prove a number wrong, Crossfoot says so and changes nothing.

        What it cannot do

        • The sums cannot see two errors that cancel each other out.
        • A row with one printed sum can only be proven wrong when that sum breaks.
        • A scan with no printed totals cannot be checked.
        • A person still has to confirm each finding, and none has been confirmed yet.
        These are transcription slips in a volunteer dataset. Each county's certified scan is the source of truth. Sources: the OpenElections sources and data repositories for the 2024 Texas general election.
