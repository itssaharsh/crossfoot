# T06b Frame the sheet, add the loupe, and make the depth read — M8
Context: T06a built the stage and it works, but its screenshots (`qa/t06a/`) show what a first-time viewer cannot get past: the sheet is pushed off to one side and clipped by the stage (selecting a value translates it to the stage centre at a fixed zoom), a third of the stage is empty, and no printed figure on the scan can be read at that size. The idea of the page is "two printed sums cross at one value, and you can add it up yourself", so the viewer has to see the whole sheet with the crossing AND read the real digits. This task fixes the framing and adds a loupe. Same worktree and branch (`site`); other sessions are still changing `crossfoot/` on `main`. BASE: the current HEAD.
Read first: AGENTS.md, UI-SPEC.md (sections 5, 7, 12, 14), site/stage.js, site/styles.css, site/app.js, site/panel.js, crossfoot/sitebuild.py, .prod-build/reports/T06a.md, and open `qa/t06a/01-opening.png`, `03-failing.png` and `04-flat.png` to see the problem.

Done when (REQUIRED): at 1440x900 with no input the whole sheet is inside the stage, tilted in depth, with the row band and the column band crossing at the selected value; above it a loupe shows the real scan pixels around that value large enough to read (printed digits at least 16 px tall on screen) with the same two bands and the ring, joined to the value on the sheet by a thin leader line; selecting another value (on the sheet, in the loupe, or from the list) moves all of it; nothing on the sheet is clipped by the stage in the tilted or the flat view; `uv run pytest -q` passes.

The composition of the stage (at 1440x900 the stage is about 946 by 756 px):
- The sheet zone is the lower part of the stage (about 62 percent of its height). The whole page is always inside it with at least 24 px clear on every side: measure the sheet's projected box (`getBoundingClientRect` after the transform) and set the scale from that, again on resize, on page change and on Lay flat. Tilted view: `perspective: 1800px` on the stage, the sheet at about `rotateX(46deg) rotateZ(-3deg)` (a sheet lying on a desk, seen from the chair; try 40 to 50 and keep what reads best), its near edge parallel to the bottom of the stage. Flat view: no rotation, fitted the same way.
- Selecting a value no longer drags the sheet to the centre. The sheet answers with a small turn only: its z-rotation moves by at most 2 degrees toward the value and it slides by at most 40 px, eased over 600 ms, and it never leaves the sheet zone. The pointer lean from T06a stays (at most 3 degrees).
- The loupe is docked at the top centre of the stage, in the space above the sheet: a paper rectangle about 560 by 230 px with a 1 px ink border and a soft contact shadow (it is a second object above the desk, not glass: no blur, no gradient, no glow). It shows the scan itself around the selected value at a scale where a printed digit is at least 16 px tall: the value's column with one column on each side, and the block's rows from EARLY down to the small totals, so the column's whole sum can be read in it. The row band and the column band are drawn in it in the same colours, and the ring on the value. A value in the loupe can be clicked to select it. When the selection moves inside the same block the loupe's content slides to the new place (350 ms); across blocks or pages it cross-fades (150 ms). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it changes at once.
- A leader line (1.5 px, ink at 60 percent, round caps) runs from the bottom edge of the loupe to the ring on the sheet. It is an SVG overlay in stage coordinates; recompute its end from the ring's `getBoundingClientRect` on every frame while the sheet is moving, and on resize.
- The loupe needs sharper pixels than the 1,600 px page image. In `crossfoot/sitebuild.py` also write one image per block cut from the page at 300 dpi (`site/data/blocks/<county>-<block id>.webp`, grey, with the block's own box recorded in the county JSON so the loupe can place values), and load a block's image only when a value in it is selected. Keep the 1,600 px page images for the sheet. Tested: every block has an image entry whose pixel size matches its box at 300 dpi within a pixel.
- The blotter: one inset line (1 px, `--line`) 12 px inside the stage's edge with 8 px corners, nothing else. The sheet keeps its page stack, its contact shadow and its dimmer far edge; check the stack reads at the new tilt (edges of the pages under it visible along the near and right edges).
- Controls become one slim row at the bottom-left of the stage, on the blotter and clear of the sheet: the filled `Replay the audit` button (still disabled in this task; put its one-line reason in a `title` and in an `aria-describedby` text of 12 px beside the row, not in a card), then `Lay flat` / `Tilt sheet`, `Previous page`, `Next page`, `Page 1 of 6`. No dark card over the sheet.
- The tape reads as paper: `--sheet` background, a 1 px shadow line under it, and a torn lower edge cut with `clip-path` (a fine zigzag, 6 px deep). Keep its type and timing.
- The panel must not waste the first screen: at 1440x900 the tape, the selected-value card and at least four rows of the list are visible without scrolling the panel. Tighten spacing, and shorten the row tape as UI-SPEC says, before shrinking any type (nothing under 12 px).
