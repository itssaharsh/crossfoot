Its centre is the scanned sheet standing in depth, on which any value can be selected to see the two printed sums that cross at it and the arithmetic on a tape. The data (`crossfoot/sitebuild.py`, a new module with `python -m crossfoot.sitebuild`; do not edit `crossfoot/cli.py` or `crossfoot/outputs.py` in this task):
- reads `runs/<run_id>/<county>/county.json` and writes `site/data/index.json` (run id, date, counties, and the summary figures the run recorded) and `site/data/<county>.json`: pages (number, image path, the image's pixel width and height, and the scale from the run's 200 dpi boxes to image pixels), blocks, values (cells and printed totals alike: page, block, row kind, column, value, status, box), checks (kind, key, ok, printed, computed, and its terms as references to values), and the events. Keep it small: no indentation, short keys are fine if documented at the top of the module.
- renders each page of the scan (`data/scans/<county>.pdf`, already in this worktree; `crossfoot.render` has the pdftoppm wrapper) to an image at most 1600 px wide, WebP or PNG, under `site/data/pages/`.
- tested with a small made-up county.json: every check's terms resolve to values, boxes are scaled, a missing box is null and never a zero box.

The page (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules; no framework, no bundler, no npm dependency; files `site/index.html`, `site/styles.css`, `site/app.js` plus a few modules, `site/favicon.svg`):
- Layout at 1440: top bar 52 px (`Crossfoot` wordmark with the mark, then the descriptor from the copy deck, county tabs at the right when there is more than one county); below it the stage (flexible, at least 760 px) and a panel 420 px wide, 24 px gutter and page padding. Tokens exactly as UI-SPEC.md section 14. Public Sans for text, Courier Prime for figures (Google Fonts, with system fallbacks). Check each text and background pair for 4.5:1 and fix the muted ink first if one fails.
- The sheet: the page image in a container with `perspective: 1800px`; the sheet itself transformed `rotateX(34deg) rotateZ(-3deg)`, with `transform-style: preserve-3d`. It is the only white surface and the brightest thing on screen. Depth has to read as a real object, without decoration: a soft contact shadow on the blotter that follows the sheet's outline; the county's other pages lying under it as a stack whose edges show (1 to 2 px offsets per page, at most five drawn); the far edge slightly dimmer than the near one. No gradients on the paper, no glow, no glass.
- Marks are one SVG overlay in the image's pixel coordinates, so they tilt with the sheet and stay sharp. Every value that has a box is a transparent, focusable rectangle (hover and focus show a 2 px outline in sheet ink). Selecting one (pointer or Enter) does this, in order (UI-SPEC section 7, T-01 and T-02): the sheet's transform eases so that value sits at the optical centre of the stage (600 ms); a band is drawn along its row from the row's label to the row's printed total (6 px, 35 percent opacity, 350 ms), then one along its column down to the small total; `--data-holds` when that sum holds, `--data-fails` when it fails; a ring closes on the value where they cross (180 ms). A printed total can be selected too, and shows the sums it sits on.
- Pointer depth: while the pointer is over the stage the sheet leans toward it by at most 3 degrees on each axis, eased, and returns when it leaves. Off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and on touch.
- Controls at the bottom-left of the stage: the one filled button `Replay the audit` (present and disabled in this task, with the line "Replay comes with the run's events" under it; the next task wires it), then quiet text buttons `Lay flat` / `Tilt sheet`, `Previous page`, `Next page`, and the page number. Changing page slides the top sheet off the stack and brings the next one up (400 ms; instant under reduced motion). `?flat=1` starts flat; under 900 px wide and under reduced motion the sheet starts flat.
- The tape (panel, top): a white paper strip, Courier Prime, right-aligned figures. For the selected value it prints the column sum line by line (label and figure for each term, a rule, `adds to` and the computed figure, then `printed total` and the printed figure, then `matches` or `does not match` with a tick or a cross), 60 ms a line, at most 900 ms; then the row sum the same way, shortened as UI-SPEC says for long sums (the first three terms, "… 14 more", the last term). Instant under reduced motion.
- Under the tape, a card for the selected value: its label in words (contest, row label as printed on the scan, precinct), the value in large figures, its status in words (`accepted`: "Both printed sums hold."; `cannot_verify`: "A printed sum through this value does not hold. Not verified."), and how it was read (model, tries) in small type.
- Under that, a list "Sums that do not hold" built from the checks that fail (rows, not cards: page, contest and row or precinct, computed against printed). It is one tab stop: arrow keys move, Enter selects the first value on that sum and turns the sheet to it. This list is the seam where the findings list goes in the next task.
- A footer line from the copy deck (limits and sources, with links to the two public repositories named in ARCHITECTURE.md).
- With no input the page opens on page 1 with a value selected whose column sum holds and whose row sum holds, drawn and printed, so the first screen already shows the idea. `?cell=<page>:<block>:<row>:<column>` selects another.
- IF a data file fails to load THEN the stage says which file (copy deck, Error) and the rest of the page still renders.
- The stage has `role="img"` with a label describing the selected value and its two sums in words; every control is a real button; focus is always visible (2 px outline in `--focus`); nothing is hover-only.

Rules that are easy to break: every number on the page comes from `site/data/`; a figure the run did not record is left out, never shown as 0. Row labels come from the data as printed on the scan; add no comment about any candidate, party or official, and write no sentence about the results themselves. Copy from UI-SPEC section 9 exactly where it has a line. No "coming soon" anywhere except the one disabled Replay line above.
