Data (`crossfoot/sitebuild.py`):

A county the run did not check (`no_printed_totals`) is listed with its reason and gets no county file beyond that.
- In the report layout the four values of a row share one box (the row's strip) and blocks have no box. For each row that carries a finding, cut a clean crop from the page at 300 dpi: the row with the contest heading, the column headings and two rows of context above and below where the page has them (no outline and no caption drawn into the image), and record in the county JSON the crop's place on the page, the row's box inside it, and, where it can be found from the ink (clusters of ink at the right end of the row, right to left, mapped through the county's column order), a box for each of the four figures; a figure that cannot be found has a null box and the row box is used. Tested on a small made-up page image.
- Page images at 1,600 px are written only for the pages that carry a finding, and page 1 of each county; other pages are listed with their numbers and no image. Report what the whole `site/data/` weighs; keep it under 25 MB.

The page:
- County tabs in the top bar, in the run's order, each with its number of disagreements in Courier Prime ("Panola 121"); a county that was not checked is a tab too, marked with a dash. `?county=<name>` selects one. Arrow keys move between tabs (one tab stop). On small screens the tabs scroll sideways inside the bar without moving the page.
- A whole-run line, quiet, on the first screen at 1440x900 and above the stage on small screens, built from `index.json` only: for example "8 counties · 7 read · 228 disagreements · 186 settled by the printed totals · 0 confirmed by a person · 4,839 printed sums: 4,737 hold with the published files, 4,803 with the corrected". Do not type any of these figures in; if the build has one county only, show nothing.
- The stage for the report layout: the page is portrait, so fit it in the sheet zone by height and keep the same tilt, stack and shadow. The selected finding's row gets one band across the row (the row's own sum: `--data-holds` when the row adds up on the scan, `--data-fails` when it does not) and, where the county has a contest total and that sum is in the finding, a band down the figure's column to the contest total row. The ring: a pencil loop round the figure when its box is known, else round the row's figures.
- The loupe shows the row's clean crop at reading size (figures at least 16 px tall), with the loop on the figure, and the tag in the blank part of the row: `public file: {value}`; for a row where two figures differ, one tag naming both ("public file: early voting 189 · election day 162"). Its caption line gives page, precinct, contest and row label.
- The card for a row: `Public file` and `Scan` each as the row's four figures in a line (total, absentee, early voting, election day; Courier Prime; the figures that differ are the strong ones), then the kind of slip and one sentence, then the status. For `cannot verify` in this layout use the finding's own reason in one plain sentence: "Both rows add up to their own total, and nothing else printed on this page can tell them apart. Left as published." followed by, when the finding records it, "The scan was read twice and both readings agree." Never say which is right.
- The tape: the row's sum with the public file's row and with the scan's row, side by side as now (four lines each, so no shortening); under it the contest column's sum both ways where the finding has it.
- The list groups by row; its lines show the part, the two values and the status as now.
- Empty county (no disagreements): the copy deck's empty line in the list, the first page on the stage with no marks, and a line from the data: "{n} of {n} printed sums hold with the published file."
- A county that was not checked: the stage shows only the sentence from the run ("Nothing on this scan can be cross-checked; left as published."), the panel is empty apart from the headline strip saying the same; Replay is disabled with that reason.
- The replay for a report county uses its own events (the sample, one step per page read, the rows that were read again, then the audit); it needs no new beats, only that a page read washes the page and a re-read row is shown in the loupe. Check its timeline builds for San Saba and Jones.
