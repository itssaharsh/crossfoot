1. The replay (UI-SPEC T-03). It replays what the run recorded, in order, nothing invented: every beat is built from an event or a figure in the county's data, and a beat whose data is missing is skipped.
- Beats, each with one caption line in plain words at the top left of the stage (13 px, on the blotter, `aria-live="polite"`):
  a. the sample: from the run's `sample` figures, for example "A sample read with claude-haiku fails 6 of 22 row sums. The county is read with claude-sonnet-4.6." The sampled blocks flash their failing sums in `--data-fails`.
  b. the read: for each `block_read` event, in order, that block on the sheet takes a light wash in `--data-holds` (10 percent) from left to right; the sheet changes page when the event's page changes. Caption: "Reading page {n} of {N}: {blocks so far} of {all} blocks".
  c. the check: the sums that failed after the first read (the `suspect` events' sums and the summary's `sums_failing_before`) draw in `--data-fails`. Caption from the figures: "{n} of {all} printed sums do not hold."
  d. each `suspect` with its `reread` events: the two failing sums draw across the sheet and cross at the value, the loupe opens on it, the ring closes; when the re-read gives another value the loupe's tag shows "first read {old} · read again {new}", and both bands turn to `--data-holds`; when the run's verdict for it is `document_inconsistent` the ring turns `--warning` and the caption gives the verdict's own figures ("Read {x} twice. The cells add to {y}. The document disagrees with itself. Listed for a person."); `cannot_verify` the same way with its reason shortened to one sentence.
  e. the audit: caption "Laid against the published file"; the findings' marks appear on the sheet page by page while the headline count in the panel counts up to its figures.
  f. the end: the page returns to its opening finding, drawn as on load.
- Pace: the whole replay takes 25 to 35 seconds at the default speed. The label reads `Replay · run of {date} · {n}x` where n is the run's recorded seconds divided by the replay's seconds, rounded, so it is true. A second control cycles the speed (1x, 2x, 4x of the default) and the label follows.
- Controls: the button reads `Pause replay` while it runs and `Resume replay` when paused; Escape or Alt+Shift+P (match `e.code`) pauses and resumes; selecting anything by hand stops the replay and leaves the page in the finished state; Alt+Shift+R resets. While it runs the list and the card are quiet (aria-busy), not hidden.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the replay steps beat by beat with no draw-on or wash animation (states change with a 150 ms fade) and the same captions; `Pause replay` still works.
- IF the county has no events THEN the button is disabled with the copy deck's reason beside it.
- Put the beat list in one small module (`site/replay.js`) driven by a plain timeline of steps built from the data, so it can be tested: a test page or a script run in the browser checks that the timeline built from the real county has the sample beat, one read step per `block_read` event, one beat per `suspect`, and the audit beat, in that order.
