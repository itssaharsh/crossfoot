What the other seven scans look like (first pages checked by eye on 11 October 2026; look at the pages yourself before writing prompts):
- Cottle, Jones, Lynn, San Saba, Scurry: an Electionware report, portrait, one precinct per section, the precinct name alone at the top left of the page (San Saba: "1", "2A", "2B", "3A", "3B", "4"; others "PRECINCT 101" or "Precinct 1"); a section can run over several pages. A Statistics block (registered voters, ballots cast, a turnout percentage), then one block per contest with a heading and "Vote For 1". Each candidate is a row: a party prefix and name (for example "REP Ted Cruz"), then four numbers under the headings TOTAL, Absentee, Early Voting, Election Day. Jones and Lynn also print "Total Votes Cast", "Overvotes" and "Undervotes" rows per contest; Cottle, San Saba and Scurry print "Write-In Totals" rows.
- Foard: a different vendor's "Precinct Report", landscape. Columns are Absentee, Early, Election, Total, each a count followed by a percentage, with "Cast Votes", "Over Votes" and "Under Votes" rows per contest. Ignore the percentages.
- Limestone: a plain-text report in two columns that prints only VOTES and PERCENT per candidate. It has no breakdown and no printed contest totals, and its published file has no parts either. Nothing on it can be cross-checked.
- The published files (`data/files/<county>.csv`): San Saba has 275 rows and six precincts written "Precinct 1", "Precinct 2A" and so on; office names are shortened and sometimes mistyped against the scan (the scan's "Pl 2" is "PI 2" in the file), candidates have no party prefix and no running mate, and there are rows for "Ballots Cast" and "Ballots Cast Blank". Cottle and Foard use "Precinct 101"; Jones, Lynn and Scurry "Precinct 1".
- Measured on three San Saba pages on 11 October 2026: one whole page at 200 dpi in one `claude-haiku` call came back in 7 to 9 seconds with about 1,700 prompt tokens and 900 to 1,300 completion tokens; 0 or 1 of the 10 to 20 rows on a page failed its own sum.

Acceptance (REQUIRED):
- WHEN a county is started THE SYSTEM SHALL decide its layout from its first page (`layouts/profile.py`): `matrix`, `report` with the column order it found, or `no_printed_totals`; then read the first page under that decision and reject the decision if more than a third of the sums fail (tested with fake answers)
- WHEN the layout is `no_printed_totals` (Limestone) THE SYSTEM SHALL not read the county, and report it as "nothing on this scan can be cross-checked; left as published"
- WHEN a report-layout page is read THE SYSTEM SHALL store every number as a ledger cell (block = the contest on that precinct, row kind = the row label, column = `total`, `absentee`, `early`, `election_day`) and check every row: total = absentee + early voting + election day; and, where the page prints one, every contest total (the candidates' totals add to "Total Votes Cast" or "Cast Votes")
- WHEN a row's sum fails THE SYSTEM SHALL hand it to the loop: re-read the row's crop at a higher dpi, one step up the ladder per failed re-read, at most six actions, then a verdict (`document_inconsistent` or `cannot_verify`) with a reason
- The reader is chosen by the same measured sample as for Panola (the first page with `claude-haiku`; more than a tenth of its row sums failing means `claude-sonnet-4.6`)
- How many sums a value sits on decides what can be settled, so say it per county. In this layout a value sits on its row's own sum (total = absentee + early voting + election day) and, only where the page prints a contest total row ("Total Votes Cast", "Cast Votes"), on that column's sum too (the candidates' figures in a column add to the contest total's figure in that column). Record for each county whether the second sum exists.
- The audit's rule stays "the printed sums decide" (`audit.py`), with one thing that a single sum cannot do: IF a published row differs from the scan's row in two or more values AND the published row adds up to its own total AND no contest total covers it THEN no printed total can tell the two rows apart: the status is `cannot_verify` with that reason, nothing is corrected, and the scan's row is read once more one step up the ladder so the finding can say whether two readings of the scan agree. A published row that fails its own total while the scan's row holds is settled against the file (tested, both cases, plus a row covered by a contest total).
- WHEN `crossfoot audit --county san_saba` finishes THE SYSTEM SHALL list every published row that disagrees with the scan's row, each with the scan's four numbers, the sums through it and whether each holds with the file's values and with the scan's, and a crop of the row; a published row that fails its own total is a `file_row_fails_own_total` finding; the `sums_check` block is written as for Panola
- A section that runs over a page break keeps its precinct; a contest that runs over a page break is one block
