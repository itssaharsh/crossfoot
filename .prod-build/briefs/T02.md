What Panola looks like (matrix layout, checked by eye): landscape sheets, 6 pages. Pages 1 to 5 are 1-bit scans at 400 dpi, page 6 is a grey 200 dpi image. A header row lists 19 precincts as columns (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20, 22, 26, 27, 28, 29) and ends in TOTAL. Each candidate is a block: a label at the left, three ruled rows labelled EARLY, ABSENTEE and PRECINCT, a row of small printed totals under the columns, and a grand total under the TOTAL column. Page 1 also has a registered-voters row and a ballots-cast block before the first contest. Contest names sit in a boxed line above their candidates. On page 6 some contests exist only in some precincts and the other cells are blacked out; treat a blacked-out cell as "not in this contest", not as zero. Read the header row once per page; do not assume it.

Constraints:
- Model access: the `openai` package against `LITELLM_BASE_URL` with `LITELLM_API_KEY`. First reads use `claude-haiku`. Images go in as base64 PNG data URLs. Ask for strict JSON and validate it.
- Keep calls under one a second. Retry a 429 or a timeout once after a wait.
- Cost meter: add up `usage.prompt_tokens` and `usage.completion_tokens` from every response; price them with a small table in `model.py` (clearly marked as an estimate) and also report the change in the gateway's own spend figure at the end of a run.
- Tracing as in ARCHITECTURE.md: `neatlogs.init(api_key=os.environ["NEATLOGS_API_KEY"], workflow_name="crossfoot")`, `neatlogs.wrap(OpenAI(...))`, `@neatlogs.span(kind="WORKFLOW" | "AGENT" | "TOOL", ...)`, `with neatlogs.trace(name, kind="TOOL") as span: span.set_attribute(...)`, then `neatlogs.flush()` and `neatlogs.shutdown()` at exit. Tag spans with run, county, page and block. Docs: https://docs.neatlogs.com/sdk/python and https://docs.neatlogs.com/guides/custom-orchestration. Run `uv run python -m neatlogs doctor --local --json` once and record the result.
- Block finding (`segment.py`) works from the image: bands of ink separated by blank bands are blocks; inside a block, columns of dark pixels that run the height of the ruled rows are the column lines. Test it on a small ruled image drawn in the test. Look at the real rendered pages while you work (you can open the PNGs), but keep model calls for reading only.
- A whole Panola read is about 45 block crops. Do not run it more than three times in this task. Check `crossfoot budget` before and after and write both figures in the report.
