1. Ring the ink, not the cell. In `qa/t06b/01-opening.png` the ellipse round the cell cuts through the digits, because figures are printed low and right-aligned in their cells; under a small total it sits low.
- In `crossfoot/sitebuild.py`, for every value with a box, find the ink box: inside the value's box in the 300 dpi block image, shrunk by a few pixels so ruled lines are left out, the bounding box of pixels darker than a threshold. Store it with the value (null when no ink is found; a zero is ink). Tested on a small made-up image: a figure printed low and right in a ruled cell gives its own box, an empty cell gives null.
- In the loupe the ring becomes a pencil loop round the ink box with 8 px of air: one closed SVG path, slightly irregular like a hand-drawn loop (a fixed shape scaled to the box, not random), 2.5 px stroke, drawn on over 180 ms from its top left; `--data-holds` or `--data-fails` by the rule already in use (fails when any sum through the value fails). On the sheet the small ring stays, centred on the ink box. With no ink box, ring the cell box as a rounded rectangle.
- Give the loupe's view 10 px of clear paper above the block's first row, so the top rule is not cut.

2. States (`?state=`), each exactly as UI-SPEC sections 5 and 9 say, and each also reached for real when its condition holds:
- `loading`: a scan line crosses the empty sheet while "Loading run…" counts files; the panel shows quiet placeholders of the tape and the card (no shimmer loop under reduced motion).
- `error`: the sheet area says which file failed, with the copy deck's line; the rest of the page renders.
- `empty`: the list says "No disagreements in this county." (for now it replaces "Sums that do not hold" when none fail).
- `partial`: no page image, so the loupe's block image is shown flat in the sheet zone instead of the sheet, with the same tape and card.
- `first-run`: no data built: the copy deck's first-run line and nothing else in the stage.
